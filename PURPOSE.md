# Purpose

## What we are trying to achieve

One agent experience, whatever provider is behind it.

Contact centres run on many providers: voice platforms, chat platforms, mail platforms. Each has
its own desktop, its own words and its own way of doing the same work. This protocol is the common
ground between them. Every provider speaks it through an adapter, and Omni draws and drives all of
them the same way, so an agent learns one desk and works every provider from it.

## How we decide

**Unify, don't restrict.** Providers will differ, and there will always be lines that are tricky
to draw. The work is to find what they share and express it once, cleanly. It is not to imagine
every way a provider might differ and design rules against each one.

**Optimise for the agent's experience.** A shape earns its place by making the desk simpler and
the same across providers, not by covering a case nobody has met.

**Omni fills the gaps.** Where a provider falls short, or where a choice is about how something
looks or behaves for the person, Omni's own preferences decide, and Omni applies them to
everything it shows, provider activity included.

**One way to do a thing.** What the protocol does is done only through the protocol. A desk may
have its own entry point, but never a different behaviour for the same function.

**Accept some shortcomings.** A unified experience with a few gaps is better than a precise one
that is different everywhere.
